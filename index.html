NASA data we use

  • Soil wetness (GWETROOT): how wet the top 1 m of soil is, from 0 (dry) to 1 (saturated). Source: NASA POWER, from NASA's MERRA-2 model.
  • Rainfall (PRECTOTCORR): daily rainfall in mm. Source: NASA POWER.
  • Maximum temperature (T2M_MAX): daily highest air temperature, 2 m above the ground. Source: NASA POWER.
  • Rain forecast: rain expected over the next 3 days. Source: Open-Meteo (not NASA data).

We use the last 90 days of daily data for each district.

Limits

  • NASA POWER data covers areas about 50 km wide, so it estimates conditions for a region, not an individual field.
  • We flag conditions linked to salt build-up; we don't measure salt or soil nutrients.
  • The most recent few days of NASA data may not be available yet.
  • Our rules are simple and meant for exploring options, not final advice. Check with a local agriculture expert before making decisions.
